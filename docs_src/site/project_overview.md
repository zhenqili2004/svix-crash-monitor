# Project Overview

## Case Study - SVIX and the Probability of a Crash

This case study rebuilds two of Ian Martin's option-implied measures for the
S&P 500, the SVIX volatility index and the probability of a 20% drop, from
the settlement prices of E-mini S&P 500 options on CME Globex, and runs them
as a daily monitor on GitHub Actions. A second part applies Martin and Shi's
bounds on the probability that a single stock crashes to Apple and AIG,
from the OptionMetrics volatility surface.
