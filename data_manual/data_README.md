# Manually created data

This folder holds data that cannot be regenerated by running `doit`, which is
why it is kept under version control. Everything the pipeline can pull again
lives in `_data/`, which is gitignored.

## `martin_2017/svix2.csv` and `martin_2017/crashprob.csv`

The series Ian Martin publishes with *What is the Expected Return on the
Market?* (Quarterly Journal of Economics, 2017), downloaded from
https://personal.lse.ac.uk/martiniw/ on October 19, 2026 (`SVIX2.xls`,
`crashprob.xls`; the description is `WIERdata.pdf` on the same page) and
converted from Excel to CSV without other changes.

- `svix2.csv`: daily SVIX² at horizons of 1, 2, 3, 6, and 12 months
  (columns `m1` to `m12`), January 4, 1996 to January 31, 2012. SVIX² is the
  annualized simple variance swap rate, equation (12) of the paper; the
  index in VIX units is 100 times its square root.
- `crashprob.csv`: the probability of a 20% drop in the S&P 500 at the same
  five horizons, as perceived by an investor with log utility who holds the
  market (the paper's Result 2), averaged by month, January 1996 to January
  2012.

The notebooks plot these as the paper's own picture, and they are the
benchmarks for the replication: the single-name part computes SVIX and the
crash probability from the OptionMetrics surface and checks them against
`svix2.csv` and `crashprob.csv`. (CME computes the same simple-variance
construction as its CVOL indexes, but publishes none for the S&P 500.)
